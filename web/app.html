Get started

Three things, once.

An account to be, an extension to encrypt with, and a name so people can reach you.

  1. An account

    Sign in with Google. Circle creates a wallet only you hold the key to.

  2. The extension

    Your files are encrypted on your own machine, by a key this page is never allowed to see. That key lives in the extension.

    Load extension/ unpacked at chrome://extensions

  3. A name

    People send to your name, not to a key. Claiming it publishes your encryption key in its records — that is how anyone finds where to encrypt.

    .pinesign.eth

You are set up. Open the extension to send a file.
