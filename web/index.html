PineSign

Encrypted here. Proven there.

Send a file so that both of you can prove it happened. It is encrypted on your machine before it leaves, stored on Swarm only as long as it needs to be, and the record of delivery is written once and never editable — not by us, not by you.

How a transfer works

  1. Alice encrypts on her own machine

    The extension derives a key from her private key and Bob's public one. Nothing readable leaves her computer, and no key is ever transmitted.

  2. The gateway pays for the storage

    It uploads the ciphertext to Swarm and covers the postage. Bob and Alice never touch a stamp, a token, or a node — and the gateway never sees the file.

  3. Bob claims it, or it disappears

    He decrypts, and proves he did by revealing something only the plaintext yields. If he never claims, the blob and its link expire together. Nothing to delete.
